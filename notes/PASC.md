# PASC — Platform for Advanced Scientific Computing Conference

**Website:** [pasc-conference.org/editions/pasc26](https://pasc-conference.org/editions/pasc26/)
**Tier:** Regular | **Tier-1:** Yes

## Overview

Interdisciplinary HPC conference co-sponsored by ACM, bridging domain science, applied mathematics, and computer science around high-performance computing. Strong scientific-computing and co-design focus. Held annually in Europe (often Switzerland) in early summer.

**PASC26 theme:** *Building Trust in Science through HPC Co-Design.*
**PASC26 dates:** June 29 – July 1, 2026.

## Submission Details

- **Abstract / paper submission:** December 12, 2025
- **Full paper submission (NO EXTENSIONS):** February 4, 2026
- **Review notifications:** March 20, 2026
- **Paper revisions due:** April 24, 2026
- **Acceptance notifications:** following revisions
- Conference: June 29 – July 1, 2026

## Review Process

- Papers go through a single review round with a revision opportunity (revisions due after the initial review notifications).
- Hard full-paper deadline — organizers explicitly state no extensions.

## Misc Notes

- Accepted papers are published in the ACM proceedings.
- Good fit for HPC co-design, scientific applications, and methods work spanning domain science and CS.
