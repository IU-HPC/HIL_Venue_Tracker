# SIGOPS ATC — ACM SIGOPS Annual Technical Conference

**Website:** [atc.sigops.org](https://atc.sigops.org/)
**Lab scope:** Core | **Venue type:** Conference

## Overview

Broad systems conference covering OS, storage, networking, runtime, parallel, distributed, cloud, and related systems topics. ATC became an ACM SIGOPS conference in 2026, continuing the USENIX ATC community and scope.

## Submission Details

- The inaugural ACM edition used a June paper deadline and a November conference.
- Long and short research papers and an operational systems track were offered in 2026.
- The 2026 process required a two-page extended abstract alongside the full paper.
- Double-blind review.

## Review Process

- Double-blind, with an initial extended-abstract screening round in the 2026 process.

## Misc Notes

- The 2027 edition is not announced; projected dates in the tracker are based on the new 2026 ACM cycle.
- ACM ATC papers are open access. Prior USENIX ATC proceedings remain available through USENIX.
- Good venue for systems work that spans OS, storage, and HPC.
