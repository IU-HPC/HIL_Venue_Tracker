# SIGMOD — ACM Special Interest Group on Management of Data

**Website:** [2026.sigmod.org](https://2026.sigmod.org)
**Tier:** Regular | **Tier-1:** No (not HPC-focused; kept for cross-domain tracking)

## Overview

Top venue for data management research, co-located with PODS (Principles of Database Systems). Not HPC-focused but tracked for cross-domain awareness. Conference held mid-June.

## Submission Details

- **Four rounds per year**, abstract due ~1 week before the paper. All four rounds for a given conference fall in the *previous* calendar year (e.g. SIGMOD 2026 rounds all close during 2025).
- Conference: mid-June
- Double-blind review

## Review Process

- Double-blind, with an author feedback phase and an interactive discussion phase before notification.
- Outcomes can be accept / reject / **revision** — a revised-paper round follows the first notification.

## Misc Notes

- Rolling round system means you can submit up to 4 times per year.
- The tracker records the four paper-submission deadlines (the day-17 dates).
